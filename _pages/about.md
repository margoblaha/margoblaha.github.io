## In brief
Margo Blaha is a PhD student at the Centro Agricoltura, Alimenti, Ambiente (C3A), University of Trento, working in vector ecology. Her research focuses on the tiger mosquito, *Aedes albopictus*, and on how climate and humans shape where it thrives. She also writes about sociology, psychology, media and culture, and the ways people understand one another.
